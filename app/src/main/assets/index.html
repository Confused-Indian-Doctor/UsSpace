Health data privacy
UsSpace requests read-only Health Connect access. Imported values are stored locally in this app. They are not uploaded, sold, or automatically shared with your partner. You can revoke Health Connect access at any time in Android Settings.
Wellness only
UsSpace does not diagnose conditions or monitor emergencies. Heart rate, SpO₂ and other measurements can be useful context, but they are not a substitute for medical assessment.
